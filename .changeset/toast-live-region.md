Add `@agencecinq/toast` (`<cinq-toast>`) for live-region notifications. Consumer sets `role="alert"` or `role="status"`. Opt-in auto-dismiss via `data-duration`; pause/resume is consumer-owned (`pause()` / `resume()`). Export `EVENTS.TOAST_OPEN` / `EVENTS.TOAST_CLOSE` from utils.
