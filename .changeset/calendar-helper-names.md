The exported helpers are named after what they convert: `toDayString()` is `dayFromDate()`, `fromDayString()` is `dateFromDay()`, and `getWeekStart()` is `getFirstDay()`, like the `firstDay` option and the `first-day` attribute. The `getWeekdays()` method is `getWeekdayNames()`, and `renderHeader()` and `renderCalendar()` take the year before the month.
